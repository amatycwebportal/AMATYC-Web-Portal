# AMATYC Web Portal

A portal where college faculty score AMATYC rubrics, and leads review the results and run consensus meetings.

## People

**User**:
A faculty member who scores assigned rubrics for their institution.
_Avoid_: Respondent, member

**College lead**:
A person who manages the Users of one institution and runs that institution's consensus meetings.
_Avoid_: Local leader, local lead

**Administrator**:
A person who manages rubrics, institutions and people across all institutions.
_Avoid_: Project lead

**AMATYC admin**:
A person on the AMATYC allowlist who can permanently delete institutions and people. This is a status in addition to a role, not a role itself.
_Avoid_: Super admin

**Unaffiliated user**:
A signed-in person who belongs to no institution, so they cannot be assigned rubrics.
_Avoid_: Orphan user, guest

**Alias**:
The stable pseudonym that stands in for a person's real name wherever anonymity applies.
_Avoid_: Nickname, display name

## Rubrics

**Section**:
A named group of scales within a rubric, shown under a shared banner.
_Avoid_: Category, group

**Section description**:
Optional text shown under a Section's title, inside the same banner.
_Avoid_: Section intro, section note

**Rubric wording**:
The editable text of a rubric: its description, each Section description, each scale prompt and each level description.
_Avoid_: Rubric content, rubric copy

## Privacy

**Results context**:
Any place where scored responses are shown, loaded or exported: analytics, response exports and consensus meetings. College leads see only Aliases here; Administrators see real identities.
_Avoid_: Reporting view

**Privacy consent**:
A person's acceptance of the current privacy notice version. It is required before an account is created or an invitation is accepted.
_Avoid_: Privacy form, agreement
